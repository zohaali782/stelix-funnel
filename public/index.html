Chapter 1

Decide who she is

Before you generate a single image, write your character down. Accounts that grow have a clear point of view. People follow a personality and a vibe, and a pretty face alone is not enough.

Fill in a casting sheet like the one below. Keep it open every time you create, so every post feels like the same person.

Give her a world too. My room has the same photo wall, candles and my name in neon, so you know it's me before you see my face.

Chapter 2

Create the face

The face is your whole brand, so spend the most time here. The goal is one master reference image that you reuse for everything after this.

  1. Go to capturesai.com and create a free account.
  2. Open AI Influencer Studio. This is where you design your virtual creator.
  3. Describe the face in detail. Use your casting sheet. If you're stuck, Prompt Writer will turn a rough idea into a full prompt.
  4. Generate a few versions and compare them side by side. Choose the one you'd happily see every day for a year.
  5. Download that image and save it as your master reference. Use it as the starting point for every photo and video from now on.

The prompt that made me

Copy it, then change the hair, eyes and details to make your own character.

Close portrait of a young woman in her early twenties, long copper-red hair with soft loose waves and curtain bangs, bright green eyes, light freckles across nose and cheeks, natural skin texture with visible pores, soft blush, glossy berry lips, small silver nose ring, thin gold chain with a small round gold coin pendant, brown ribbed long-sleeve top, soft window light, blurred modern living room background, shot on a 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, candid relaxed smile, photorealistic

Stelix tip

Realism comes from small imperfections. Ask for pores, freckles, flyaway hairs and soft uneven light. Words like "flawless" and "perfect skin" make faces look plastic.

Chapter 3

Build her world

Now give your character a life. Plan your photos in one sitting and you'll have content for weeks.

  1. Write a shot list: 5 places × 5 outfits. A café, a city street, a beach, a car, a cosy room.
  2. Create lifestyle photos with AI Image Generation, starting from your master reference every time.
  3. Move her to new places with Change Background. Same face, new city.
  4. Try outfits and brand-style shots with AI Fashion Models and Lifestyle Photography.
  5. Clean anything odd with AI Objects Remover, then run your best picks through Upscale & Enhance.

Six places, one afternoon. None of these trips happened.

A full product shoot in one session

Brands want more than one nice photo. With AI Fashion Models and E-Commerce Models you can shoot a whole set around one piece of clothing:

  • Front view and full look
  • A close-up of the product detail
  • Side profile and movement
  • A skin close-up that proves the realism

That's a complete product page, with no model, studio or sample shipped.

Keep her consistent

  • Keep your three fixed traits in every prompt, word for word.
  • Reuse the same lighting words ("soft window light", "golden hour") so your feed feels like one story.
  • Delete any image where she looks like a different person. One off post can break the illusion.

Stelix tip

Save your best prompts in a note on your phone. Your prompt library becomes the recipe book for your character.

Chapter 4

Make her move

Photos build the feed, but video is what gets reach. Movement is also what makes people believe: hair moving, a head turn, a camera drifting closer.

Cinematic clips with Motion Control

  1. Pick one of your strongest photos from chapter 3.
  2. Open AI Motion Control and choose how the camera moves: a slow push-in, a gentle orbit, or a handheld walk.
  3. Keep motion small and natural. Subtle movement looks real, big movement looks like AI.
  4. Export 5 to 8 second clips. Short loops rewatch well on Reels.

Talking videos

  1. Write a short script with Script Writer. Keep it under 20 seconds.
  2. Make the first line stop the scroll with Hook Writer.
  3. Generate the talking video in AI Influencer Studio using your master reference.

Before posting, run every video through Video Upscale 4K. Sharp footage looks more expensive and more real.

Stelix tip

Motion Control is where the realism really shows. When in doubt, turn a great photo into a slow cinematic clip.

Chapter 6

Stay on Instagram's good side

AI creators are allowed on Instagram. Accounts get into trouble when they hide it or break the usual rules. Build trust from day one.

  • Label your AI content. Turn on Instagram's AI label when you post realistic AI images or video.
  • Say it in your bio. Something like "AI creator ✦ made with CapturesAI" keeps everything honest.
  • Never use a real person. No real faces, no celebrity look-alikes, no using someone else's name.
  • No fake engagement. Skip bought followers, follow/unfollow and engagement pods.
  • Grow gradually. Post daily, but don't mass-DM or mass-follow from a brand new account.
  • Keep captions honest. Don't claim she went somewhere or used a product in real life.

Stelix tip

Being open about AI is part of the appeal. My most saved posts are the ones that show how I'm made.

Once you post consistently, these are the ways an AI creator earns.

Affiliate links

Digital products

Your prompts are valuable. Package them as prompt packs, content calendars or caption templates.

Her own product line

Once people know her, put her name on a product. Mock up the packaging with your character's name and test interest before you make anything.

Your own funnel
